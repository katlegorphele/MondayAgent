# Cover Letter Writer

Write a compelling, tailored cover letter for a specific job application. Address the hiring manager, reference specific experience, and demonstrate genuine interest in the company and role.

## Trigger
When a job title, company, job description, and candidate CV are provided.

## Steps

1. Read the job description, noting: key requirements, company values, and the role's mission
2. Read the candidate's base CV
3. Write a 3-4 paragraph cover letter:
   - **Paragraph 1**: Express interest in the specific role and company. Mention something specific about the company.
   - **Paragraph 2**: Connect the candidate's experience to the job requirements. Reference 2-3 specific achievements or skills.
   - **Paragraph 3**: Show understanding of the industry (law/compliance/finance) and how the candidate fits.
   - **Paragraph 4**: Professional closing, enthusiasm for the opportunity, call to action.
4. Address to "Hiring Manager" or use the actual name if known

## Rules
- NEVER fabricate experience or achievements
- Keep it to 3-4 paragraphs, under 400 words
- Use professional, warm tone — not robotic, not casual
- Reference the role and company by name
- No clichés ("I am writing to apply for...", "I believe I am the perfect candidate")
- Show, don't just tell — use specific examples from the CV

## Output Format
Clean markdown. The letter should be ready to paste into an application form.
